A space should feel considered, not decorated.

Every project at Lo Spazio starts with a conversation, not a mood board. We spend time in the space before we change it — how the morning light lands, where a family actually gathers, what needs to disappear and what deserves to stay.

The result is interiors that feel inevitable: rooms that were always meant to look this way, built for the people who live in them.
